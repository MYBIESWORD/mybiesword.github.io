## 1. About MYBIE SWORD
MYBIE SWORD is the parent brand and project ecosystem built around a shared identity, vision, philosophy, and standards.

Our ambition goes beyond a single meme coin. We aim to develop progressively into a meaningful and sustainable ecosystem, with the long-term ambition of building a network of our own if technical, security, operational, and resource readiness supports it.

We believe credibility is built through actions, not promises.

## 2. Brand Identity — One Parent Brand, Distinct Projects
- **MYBIE SWORD:** Parent company and ecosystem. The official sword character emblem is the primary company identity.
- **$MYBIE:** The first cryptocurrency project under the parent brand. Its crowned penguin is a token-specific mascot, not the company logo.
- **Future projects:** May have approved supporting identities while remaining under MYBIE SWORD and its shared standards.

**The sword emblem remains the primary identity of MYBIE SWORD as the ecosystem evolves.**

## 3. Our Vision and Mission
We are building beyond meme trends: step by step, toward a meaningful and sustainable project ecosystem.

Our mission is to preserve the MYBIE SWORD identity, operate transparently and accountably, prioritize security and trust, and grow according to real capability and resources.

An independent network is a long-term ambition, not a claim that a network currently exists or is launching. Any such development requires technical feasibility, qualified capability, security review, sustainable resources, and explicit approval.

Read the [Company Vision & Mission](COMPANY_VISION_MISSION.md).
