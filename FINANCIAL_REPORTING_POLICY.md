**Effective Date:** 9 November 2026  
**Policy Owner:** Founder  
**Approval Status:** Prepared under the Founder-approved Financial Governance framework

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the standards and procedures for preparing, reviewing, reconciling, and distributing MYBIE SWORD financial reports.

Its purpose is to ensure that financial information is accurate, traceable, clearly classified, and presented consistently to the Founder, authorized team members, and the public where disclosure has been approved.

This policy supplements the **Financial Governance Policy (MS-FIN-001)**. If there is a conflict, the Financial Governance Policy and the Founder's final direction take precedence.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to financial reporting for MYBIE SWORD, including:

- Project income and Creator Fee proceeds.
- Operational and project-development expenses.
- Transfers between designated project wallets.
- Wallet balances and transaction reconciliation.
- Team and Founder compensation records.
- Internal and public financial reports.

This policy governs reporting and recordkeeping. It does not grant the Finance Department or any team member authority to initiate, sign, approve, or execute blockchain transactions.

## 3. Reporting Responsibility

The Finance Department is responsible for preparing and maintaining MYBIE SWORD financial reports.

The Finance Department must:

1. Collect available financial records and supporting evidence.
2. Record income, expenses, and wallet transfers accurately.
3. Reconcile recorded transactions and balances against available blockchain and payment evidence.
4. Identify incomplete, pending, disputed, or unverified items.
5. Prepare internal reports and public-report drafts as applicable.
6. Submit reports and material discrepancies to the Founder.
7. Correct reports when verified errors are identified and preserve an appropriate record of the correction.

The Founder retains final authority over financial decisions and approval of reports for public release.

## 4. Reporting Frequency and Period

MYBIE SWORD requires financial reporting on a monthly basis.

Each monthly report should clearly identify the reporting month and the date on which the report was prepared. If information is received after a report has been prepared, the Finance Department should identify whether the information requires an amended report or inclusion in the next reporting period.

This policy does not impose a specific submission day or deadline. The Founder may set reporting deadlines and request additional reports when needed.

## 5. Minimum Contents of an Internal Monthly Report

The internal monthly report should include the following sections, where information is available:

### 5.1 Reporting Summary

- Reporting period.
- Preparation date.
- Prepared by.
- Review or reconciliation status.
- Important notes, limitations, and unresolved items.

### 5.2 Opening and Closing Balances

Show the opening and closing recorded balances for each designated project wallet, using the relevant asset and unit.

Where practical, distinguish token quantities from estimated fiat values. State the valuation source and timestamp when a fiat conversion is used. Do not present an estimate as a confirmed realized amount.

### 5.3 Income

Record project income with, where available:

- Date received.
- Income category and description.
- Amount and asset.
- Receiving wallet or account label.
- Transaction signature or payment reference.
- Verification status.

Creator Fee proceeds must be recorded first as received by the Launch Wallet. Their initial receipt must not be confused with a later transfer to another project wallet.

### 5.4 Expenses

Record each expense with, where available:

- Date paid.
- Expense category and business purpose.
- Amount and asset.
- Paying wallet or payment source.
- Recipient or vendor information, subject to access restrictions.
- Transaction signature or payment reference.
- Invoice, receipt, or other supporting evidence.
- Approval status.

Expenses should be categorized consistently so that operational costs, development costs, team payments, and other project costs can be distinguished.

### 5.5 Transfers Between Project Wallets

Record each transfer separately from income and expenses to avoid double-counting.

Each transfer record should include:

- Transfer date.
- Source wallet label.
- Destination wallet label.
- Amount and asset.
- Transaction signature or explorer reference.
- Stated purpose.
- Verification status.
- Available approval and supporting evidence.

Transfers from the Launch Wallet to the Operational Wallet or Project Wallet are determined manually by the Founder. The Finance Department records and reconciles those transfers but does not decide the allocation, timing, or amount.

### 5.6 Compensation and Owner Distributions

Founder compensation, team compensation, and owner profit distributions must be recorded in their appropriate separate categories.

Individual compensation details and owner distribution details are private/internal information. They must not be included in a public report unless the Founder explicitly approves the specific disclosure.

### 5.7 Reconciliation and Exceptions

The report must identify:

- Transactions matched to available evidence.
- Transactions awaiting confirmation.
- Differences between recorded balances and observed balances.
- Missing receipts or transaction references.
- Failed, reversed, disputed, or otherwise unusual transactions, where applicable.
- Corrective actions or follow-up required.

Unresolved discrepancies must be disclosed to the Founder. They must not be concealed or silently adjusted.

## 6. Verification Standards

Financial entries must be supported by reliable evidence appropriate to the transaction.

For blockchain transactions, the Finance Department should use the transaction signature and a suitable blockchain explorer or other reliable on-chain record to verify the transaction details.

A wallet screenshot alone may not establish that a transaction was completed. Where possible, transaction records should be checked against the relevant on-chain data.

For off-chain payments, supporting evidence may include invoices, receipts, payment confirmations, or other relevant records.

Each entry should be identified as one of the following, as applicable:

- **Verified:** supported by evidence and reconciled.
- **Pending Verification:** evidence is incomplete or confirmation is outstanding.
- **Discrepancy:** available records do not agree or require investigation.

The report must state the basis for any material estimate or valuation.

## 7. Internal and Public Reports

MYBIE SWORD maintains two reporting audiences.

### 7.1 Internal Financial Reports

Internal reports are intended for the Founder and authorized team members according to their assigned access.

They may contain detailed income and expense records, wallet-level reconciliation, transaction references, budgets, compensation information, payment evidence, and unresolved financial matters.

Access must be limited to people who need the information for their responsibilities.

### 7.2 Public Financial Reports

Public reports are summaries prepared for external transparency.

They may include approved summaries of project income, expenses, general fund usage, development progress, and other information the Founder has authorized for disclosure.

Public reports must not expose private/internal or strictly confidential information. Wallet addresses are not automatically public; each proposed disclosure of a wallet address requires the Founder's explicit decision.

A public report must not contain unverified figures presented as confirmed financial results.

## 8. Review and Publication Approval

The reporting workflow is:

1. The Finance Department collects and records available financial information.
2. The Finance Department reconciles the records and identifies exceptions.
3. The report is reviewed for completeness, accuracy, and appropriate information classification.
4. The report is submitted to the Founder.
5. The Founder reviews the report and may request clarification or correction.
6. A public version may be published only after the Founder explicitly approves that version.

Preparation or internal circulation of a draft does not constitute approval for public release.

## 9. Corrections and Updated Reports

If an error or omission is discovered, the Finance Department must investigate it and notify the Founder when material.

Corrections should preserve traceability. Where a report has already been distributed, the corrected version should identify that it supersedes or amends the earlier version and explain the correction at an appropriate level of detail.

Original records and supporting evidence should not be silently deleted or altered to hide a previous entry.

## 10. Recordkeeping and Access Control

The Finance Department must keep financial reports and supporting records organized and accessible to authorized reviewers.

Records should be stored in appropriate access-controlled locations. Public GitHub documentation must not contain seed phrases, private keys, passwords, recovery codes, authentication secrets, or confidential personal payment information.

Transaction references may be included in public reports only after review and approval. Internal records must retain sufficient evidence to support reconciliation and auditability.

## 11. No Independent Transaction Authority

The Finance Department's reporting responsibilities do not confer authority to move project funds.

No report, spreadsheet, reconciliation, or recommendation is itself an authorization to transfer assets, sign a transaction, change wallet settings, or make a payment.

All such actions remain subject to the Founder's explicit authorization and the applicable project security procedures.

## 12. Policy Amendments

The Founder has final authority to approve amendments to this policy.

Each approved amendment should be recorded with an updated version number, effective date, and a brief description of the change.
