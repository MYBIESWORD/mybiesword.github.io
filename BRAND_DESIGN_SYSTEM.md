# MYBIE SWORD — Brand Design System

## 1. Purpose
This document sets practical visual rules for consistent use of the MYBIE SWORD parent brand and its token-specific supporting identities.

## 2. Brand Architecture
- **MYBIE SWORD:** The official sword character emblem is the primary company and ecosystem identity.
- **$MYBIE:** The crowned penguin is the token-specific mascot and must remain visually subordinate to the parent-brand identity when both appear together.
- **Future projects:** May have their own approved identities, but remain under the MYBIE SWORD parent brand and shared standards.

## 3. Primary Company Logo
The approved MYBIE SWORD logo is the sword character emblem supplied and approved by the Founder. Its recognizable artwork includes the MYBIE-marked black headband, expressive silver sword character, flowing black cape, ornate sword, golden moon, castle-like setting, $MYBIE flag, gold circular frame, and MYBIE SWORD wordmark.

Use the exact approved source artwork wherever possible. Do not substitute a penguin or another supporting character for this logo.

## 4. Color Direction
| Color family | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Black | Primary backgrounds, depth, and cape/headband identity |
| Gold | Signature highlight, emblem framing, prestige, and emphasis |
| Silver | Sword character and metallic contrast |
| Purple | Supporting accent associated especially with $MYBIE token artwork |

The exact approved artwork is the source of truth. **Official hexadecimal color values have not yet been formally approved.** Do not invent or publish color codes as official until sampled, reviewed, and approved by the Founder.

## 5. Typography
The logo's existing lettering is part of the artwork and should not be recreated with substitute fonts. For supporting documents and digital materials, use legible, accessible typography with clear hierarchy. A formal brand typeface has not yet been approved.

## 6. Logo Integrity
- Preserve original aspect ratio and proportions.
- Do not stretch, skew, crop essential elements, or recolor the official artwork.
- Maintain sufficient contrast and clear space.
- Do not place competing character artwork in a way that weakens the sword emblem's primary status.
- Use the original official files for official placements whenever possible.

## 7. Parent Brand and Token Co-Presentation
When both identities appear, present the MYBIE SWORD sword emblem as the parent brand and label the penguin as the $MYBIE token mascot. Keep the distinction clear in captions and layouts.

## 8. Digital and Future Network Use
The identity must remain recognizable across websites, documentation, social channels, applications, and any future network interface. Responsive layouts may change display size, but must not change the essential artwork or brand hierarchy.

## 9. Approval and Change Control
The Founder retains final approval over the official logo, mascot identity, color system, typography, and material brand changes.

## 10. Items Requiring Formal Approval
- Exact HEX/RGB/CMYK color values.
- Official supporting typefaces.
- Minimum logo size and clear-space measurements.
- Approved monochrome and small-format variants.
- Full examples of correct and incorrect logo placement.
