02 / WHAT WE STAND FOR

Principles before promises.

Every project carrying this identity is expected to respect the same foundation.

01
◇

Security

Protect people, accounts, wallets, and project infrastructure through careful controls.

02
◎

Transparency

Share material information clearly. Separate verified facts from plans and intentions.

03
✧

Honesty

Do not promise outcomes, conceal material facts, or present assumptions as certainty.

04
⬡

Responsibility

Understand the consequences of decisions and communicate changes responsibly.

05
⌑

Accountability

Keep records, accept scrutiny, correct mistakes, and explain what changed.
