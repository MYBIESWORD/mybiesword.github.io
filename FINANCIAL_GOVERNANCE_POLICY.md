## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the financial governance principles, wallet structure, approval authority, recordkeeping responsibilities, and reporting standards of MYBIE SWORD.

Its objectives are to protect project funds, maintain accurate financial records, support responsible decision-making, and provide appropriate transparency to the team and the public.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to MYBIE SWORD project funds, designated project wallets, financial activities, financial records, and personnel involved in finance, operations, or project management.

This policy does not authorize any person to access wallet credentials, sign transactions, or move funds without the required approval.

## 3. Project Wallet Structure

MYBIE SWORD uses three designated wallets with separate purposes:

| Wallet Role | Primary Purpose |
|---|---|
| Launch Wallet | Initial receiving wallet for Creator Fee proceeds. |
| Operational Wallet | Operational expenses and approved team payments. |
| Project Wallet | Project expansion, development, and other approved project-specific needs. |

The wallet roles are distinct and must not be treated as interchangeable.

### 3.1 Creator Fee Receiving and Transfers

All Creator Fee proceeds must initially remain in the Launch Wallet.

The Founder decides whether and when funds are transferred from the Launch Wallet to the Operational Wallet or Project Wallet. No fixed allocation percentage, transfer schedule, or transfer amount is established by this policy.

Every transfer must be verified and recorded, including its date, amount, source wallet, destination wallet, transaction reference, purpose, and supporting evidence where available.

The Finance Department may prepare records and recommendations but does not receive automatic authority to initiate or approve transfers.

## 4. Founder Authority

The Founder retains final decision-making authority over MYBIE SWORD financial matters, including:

- Approval of transfers and other fund movements.
- Budgets and project expenditures.
- Team compensation and Founder compensation.
- Owner profit distributions.
- Allocation of funds for operations, reserves, and development.
- Approval of public financial reports.
- Changes to this policy and related financial rules.

Team members may provide recommendations and raise concerns. Final decisions remain with the Founder.

No employee, contractor, or department may independently move project funds or represent that a financial transaction has been approved without the Founder’s authorization.

## 5. Finance Department Responsibilities

The Finance Department is responsible for financial administration and reporting. Its responsibilities include:

1. Recording all project income and expenses.
2. Maintaining an organized transaction ledger.
3. Reconciling recorded balances with available wallet and transaction evidence.
4. Recording transfers between project wallets and retaining transaction references.
5. Preparing monthly financial reports.
6. Preparing public financial summaries and internal financial reports.
7. Identifying and reporting discrepancies, missing evidence, or unverified transactions.
8. Maintaining supporting documentation in the appropriate access-controlled location.

The Finance Department is responsible for recording and reporting, not for independently authorizing or executing transactions.

## 6. Founder Compensation and Owner Profit

Founder compensation and owner profit entitlement are separate financial concepts and must be recorded separately.

### 6.1 Founder Compensation

Founder compensation relates to work, leadership, management, and operational responsibilities performed for the project. When approved and paid, it must be recorded as an operational expense.

This policy does not establish a fixed salary, amount, percentage, or payment schedule. Any compensation must be determined and approved by the Founder in consideration of available funds and project needs.

### 6.2 Owner Profit Entitlement

Owner profit entitlement arises from ownership and is separate from compensation for work.

Any owner profit distribution must be separately identified, documented, and approved. This policy does not establish a distribution amount, percentage, or schedule. Applicable legal, contractual, and ownership arrangements must be respected.

## 7. Financial Reporting

MYBIE SWORD requires monthly financial reporting.

At a minimum, reports should distinguish:

- Opening and closing recorded balances.
- Income received during the reporting period.
- Expenses paid during the reporting period.
- Transfers between project wallets.
- Funds allocated or retained for operations and development.
- Outstanding items, discrepancies, or transactions awaiting verification.

Reports must be based on available records and evidence. Estimates, pending items, and unverified transactions must be clearly identified and must not be presented as confirmed figures.

## 8. Information Classification and Access

Financial information is classified into three levels.

### 8.1 Public

Information approved for public disclosure may include:

- Summaries of project income and expenses.
- General descriptions of how project funds are used.
- High-level development allocations and project progress.
- Monthly public financial summaries.
- Transaction evidence that is safe and appropriate to disclose.

Wallet addresses are not automatically public under this policy. The Founder must explicitly decide whether each wallet address may be published.

### 8.2 Private / Internal

Internal information is limited to the Founder and team members who require access according to their responsibilities. It may include:

- Detailed financial statements and transaction records.
- Internal budgets and forecasts.
- Individual team compensation.
- Founder compensation.
- Contracts, invoices, and payment evidence.
- Wallet details and internal financial procedures.
- Non-public business and operational strategies.

Access must be limited to authorized personnel.

### 8.3 Strictly Confidential

Strictly confidential information includes:

- Seed phrases and private keys.
- Wallet passwords and recovery codes.
- Administrative credentials and authentication secrets.
- Other information that could enable unauthorized access to systems or funds.

Such information must never be published in a public repository or shared through ordinary project documentation. No person should request or disclose another person's seed phrase or private key as part of routine financial administration.

## 9. Public Financial Reporting and Approval

The Finance Department prepares public financial reports using verified records.

Before publication, each report must be reconciled, reviewed for accuracy, and checked to remove private, internal, or strictly confidential information.

The Founder must approve the final report before it is published. No draft or unapproved report may be represented as an official MYBIE SWORD financial statement.

## 10. Records and Evidence

Financial records must be organized, retained, and made available to authorized reviewers.

Where applicable, records should include:

- Transaction date and amount.
- Source and destination wallet labels.
- Transaction signature or explorer reference.
- Transaction purpose.
- Approval evidence.
- Supporting invoice, receipt, or payment documentation.

Blockchain transaction references may support verification but do not replace the project's internal accounting records.

Public repositories must contain only information approved for public release. Internal records and confidential information must be stored in access-controlled locations.

## 11. Policy Amendments

The Founder has final authority to approve amendments to this policy.

Each amendment should be documented with an updated version number, effective date, and a brief description of the change. The latest Founder-approved version is the governing version.

## 12. Governance Principle

MYBIE SWORD is committed to responsible financial management, accurate recordkeeping, controlled fund movements, and appropriate transparency.

Transparency must be supported by verifiable records and must not compromise wallet security, personal privacy, or legitimate confidential business information.
