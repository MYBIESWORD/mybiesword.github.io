# MYBIE SWORD — Visual Identity & Asset Usage Guidelines

## 1. Purpose
This document establishes the official visual hierarchy and usage rules for MYBIE SWORD and its projects. It protects the parent brand's identity and prevents token-specific characters from being mistaken for the company logo.

## 2. Official Brand Hierarchy
- **MYBIE SWORD:** Parent company and ecosystem; the sword character emblem is the official primary logo.
- **$MYBIE:** First token under the parent brand; the crowned penguin is its token-specific mascot.
- **Future projects:** May have approved supporting identities, but remain under the MYBIE SWORD parent brand.

The penguin and other supporting characters must not replace the official sword emblem in company-wide or ecosystem-wide contexts.

## 3. Primary Company Logo — MYBIE SWORD
The exact sword character artwork supplied and approved by the Founder is the official primary company identity. Its recognizable elements include:
- Silver sword character with expressive eyes.
- Black headband marked “MYBIE”.
- Flowing black cape.
- Ornate sword with gold details.
- Golden moon and castle-like landscape.
- Black flag marked “$MYBIE”.
- Glowing gold circular frame.
- MYBIE SWORD wordmark and “MORE MEMES. BIGGER DREAMS.” slogan.

Use the original approved repository asset: [assets/brand/mybie-sword-logo.png](assets/brand/mybie-sword-logo.png).

This is the primary logo for official company announcements, parent-brand documentation, ecosystem communications, governance materials, and future network-wide identity.

## 4. $MYBIE Token Mascot
The crowned penguin is the official mascot for the $MYBIE token only. Its defining traits are:
- Black cape.
- Golden crown.
- Pixel-style sunglasses.
- Sword.
- Purple and gold accents.
- A commanding, regal visual presence representing protection, command, dignity, and strength.

Use the original approved repository asset: [assets/token/mybie-coin-logo.png](assets/token/mybie-coin-logo.png).

See [TOKEN_MASCOT_GUIDELINES.md](TOKEN_MASCOT_GUIDELINES.md) for the detailed token mascot standard.

## 5. Correct Logo Selection
| Communication context | Required identity |
| --- | --- |
| MYBIE SWORD company announcement | Sword company logo |
| Ecosystem-wide documentation | Sword company logo |
| Governance and company policies | Sword company logo |
| $MYBIE token announcement | $MYBIE penguin mascot, with parent brand identified where appropriate |
| Token-specific promotional content | $MYBIE penguin mascot |
| Explaining the parent/token relationship | Both, clearly labeled |
| Future ecosystem-wide network identity | Sword company logo |

## 6. Visual Integrity
- Use the original approved artwork whenever possible.
- Preserve original proportions and aspect ratio.
- Do not stretch, distort, crop essential details, or materially recolor the artwork.
- Do not redraw, replace, or materially alter the primary logo without explicit Founder approval.
- Maintain sufficient contrast, legibility, and clear space.
- Do not create unofficial variants that could be mistaken for approved master assets.

## 7. Color and Design Direction
The established visual direction uses black, gold, and silver for the parent-brand sword identity. Purple and gold are supporting accents strongly associated with the $MYBIE token artwork.

Exact HEX/RGB/CMYK values and official typefaces have not yet been formally approved. Refer to [BRAND_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](BRAND_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md); do not invent official specifications.

## 8. Future Network and Ecosystem Continuity
If MYBIE SWORD develops its own network, the sword emblem remains the primary company and ecosystem identity. The $MYBIE penguin remains the token-specific mascot. New technology does not automatically authorize a logo or mascot replacement.

## 9. Asset Repository
Current approved asset locations:
- Company logo: assets/brand/mybie-sword-logo.png
- $MYBIE token logo: assets/token/mybie-coin-logo.png

New assets must be placed in the correct category and documented. A new design is not official until approved by the Founder.

## 10. Approval Authority
The Founder retains final approval over the parent logo, token mascot, brand hierarchy, material visual changes, and any new official identity.
