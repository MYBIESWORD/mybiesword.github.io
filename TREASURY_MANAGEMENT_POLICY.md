# TREASURY MANAGEMENT POLICY

**Document ID:** MS-FIN-003  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** 9 November 2026  
**Approval Status:** Prepared under the Founder-approved Financial Governance framework  
**Responsible Function:** Finance Department  
**Final Authority:** Founder

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the governance framework for MYBIE SWORD's project wallets, treasury records, fund movements, and wallet-related financial controls. Its purpose is to support accountability, accurate recordkeeping, and the protection of project funds.

This policy does not authorize any person or department to initiate a blockchain transaction independently.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to the three designated MYBIE SWORD project wallets and to all personnel involved in financial administration, reporting, or authorized handling of project funds.

The designated wallets are:

| Wallet | Solflare Display Name | Primary Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Launch Wallet | Launch Budget | Initial receiving wallet for Creator Fee proceeds |
| Operational Wallet | Team Compensation | Operational expenses and approved team payments |
| Project Wallet | Treasury Reserve | Project expansion, development, and project-specific needs |

Wallet addresses are maintained in the project's controlled internal records. They are not reproduced in this public policy. Publication of a wallet address requires the Founder's explicit approval.

## 3. Wallet Roles and Fund Handling

### 3.1 Launch Wallet

The Launch Wallet is the initial receiving wallet for all MYBIE SWORD Creator Fee proceeds.

Creator Fee proceeds must remain in the Launch Wallet until the Founder decides that a transfer is necessary. No automatic allocation, percentage split, transfer schedule, or minimum balance is established by this policy.

### 3.2 Operational Wallet

The Operational Wallet is designated for project operating expenses and approved team payments.

Funds may be moved into this wallet only when the Founder has decided and authorized the transfer. The Finance Department must record the purpose and supporting evidence for each movement and subsequent expense.

### 3.3 Project Wallet

The Project Wallet is designated for project expansion, development, and other project-specific needs.

Funds may be moved into this wallet only when the Founder has decided and authorized the transfer. The Finance Department must record the purpose and supporting evidence for each movement and subsequent use.

### 3.4 No Assumed Allocation

The existence of three designated wallets does not establish any fixed division of funds. The Founder determines whether a transfer is made, its amount, timing, destination, and purpose.

## 4. Authority and Transaction Approval

1. The Founder retains final authority over all project treasury decisions and transfers.
2. The Founder personally determines and carries out transfers between designated wallets when required.
3. The Finance Department is responsible for documentation, reconciliation, verification of records, and reporting. It does not have independent authority to move, withdraw, allocate, or spend project funds.
4. No team member or other representative may initiate or execute a project-wallet transaction without the Founder's explicit authorization.
5. No fixed transfer threshold, recurring transfer, automatic distribution, or spending allowance is created by this policy.
6. Any proposed change to wallet roles or treasury arrangements must be referred to the Founder for a decision.

## 5. Transfer Verification and Records

Each transfer must be documented and reconciled. The transaction record should include, where available and applicable:

- Date and time of the transaction;
- Source wallet and destination wallet;
- Amount and asset transferred;
- Transaction signature or transaction ID;
- Stated purpose and relevant project category;
- Network fee, where applicable;
- Supporting evidence and verification status; and
- Confirmation that the transaction was authorized by the Founder.

The Finance Department must use blockchain transaction records and available wallet records to reconcile transfers. A transfer must not be reported as completed until its on-chain status has been checked using reliable transaction evidence.

If a transaction cannot be verified, appears inconsistent, or has a discrepancy, the Finance Department must flag it for the Founder's review and must not present it as confirmed.

## 6. Wallet Security and Confidential Information

1. Wallet recovery phrases, private keys, passwords, authentication codes, and other security credentials are strictly confidential.
2. Such credentials must never be included in public GitHub files, public financial reports, routine team reports, or transaction records.
3. No person should request or disclose another person's recovery phrase or private key as part of ordinary financial administration.
4. Public documentation may describe wallet purposes and governance, but must not expose credentials or other security secrets.
5. Any suspected unauthorized access, compromised credential, unexpected transaction, or loss of wallet control must be reported to the Founder promptly.
6. This policy does not prescribe a particular custody product, hardware device, multisignature arrangement, or technical access configuration. Those arrangements require a separate decision by the Founder.

## 7. Finance Department Responsibilities

The Finance Department must:

- Maintain an accurate internal register of the designated project wallets;
- Record incoming Creator Fee proceeds when supported by reliable evidence;
- Record and reconcile transfers between project wallets;
- Record operational expenses and project-specific expenditure with supporting evidence;
- Distinguish transfers between project wallets from actual expenses;
- Maintain transaction references and verification status;
- Identify missing records, discrepancies, or unusual activity and report them to the Founder; and
- Include relevant treasury activity in the monthly financial reporting process.

Recording or reconciling a transaction does not grant the Finance Department authority to execute it.

## 8. Reporting and Transparency

Treasury activity must be reflected in the project's monthly internal financial reporting.

Public reporting may include verified summaries of project income, transfers, expenses, and general fund usage, subject to the Financial Governance Policy and Financial Reporting Policy.

Before any public disclosure, the Finance Department must check the accuracy of the information and remove private or security-sensitive details. The Founder must review and approve the public report before publication.

Wallet addresses and detailed wallet information must not be published merely because they appear in internal records. The Founder must explicitly approve each public disclosure.

## 9. Exceptions and Policy Changes

Any exception, change in wallet purpose, new project wallet, or change to transaction authority requires the Founder's explicit decision.

Any proposed amendment to this policy must be documented. The Finance Department may recommend changes but may not approve them independently.
