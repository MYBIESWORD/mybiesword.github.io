# $MYBIE — Official Token Mascot Identity Guidelines

## 1. Purpose and Brand Hierarchy
This document defines the mascot identity used specifically for the first token, $MYBIE.
- **MYBIE SWORD sword emblem:** The primary identity of the parent company and ecosystem.
- **$MYBIE penguin mascot:** A supporting, token-specific identity for the first coin.

The penguin is not the company logo and must never replace the official MYBIE SWORD sword emblem in parent-brand contexts.

## 2. Official Mascot Characteristics
The $MYBIE mascot is a regal penguin character with these defining features:
- Black royal cape or cloak.
- Golden crown.
- Pixel-style sunglasses.
- Sword.
- Purple and gold visual accents.

These traits form the mascot's recognizable identity and should remain consistent across token-specific creative work.

## 3. Symbolic Meaning
| Element | Intended meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Black cape | Protection, dignity, and presence |
| Golden crown | Royal aura, leadership, and authority |
| Pixel sunglasses | Meme culture and recognizable digital character |
| Sword | Command, authority, protection, and strength |
| Flame of authority | The mascot's commanding presence and force of character |
| Purple and gold accents | Distinctive token visual language, prestige, and energy |

These are brand-symbolic meanings, not claims of real-world power or authority.

## 4. Continuity Across Future Technology
The $MYBIE mascot identity is intended to remain recognizable if the ecosystem expands or MYBIE SWORD eventually develops its own network. Technology, products, and use cases may evolve. The mascot's defining characteristics must not be casually replaced or discarded. Any material redesign requires explicit Founder approval.

The mascot may be adapted for different formats, poses, campaigns, or technical environments, provided its defining identity remains recognizable and the original official asset is respected.

## 5. Correct Usage
Use the penguin mascot for $MYBIE token communications, token-specific community campaigns, coin-related creative materials, and token education or identification materials.

Do not use the penguin as a substitute for the parent company's sword logo in company-wide announcements, parent-brand documentation, ecosystem-wide governance or policy materials, or official MYBIE SWORD company identity placements.

Both identities may appear together when clearly explaining the relationship between MYBIE SWORD and $MYBIE.

## 6. Asset Integrity and Approval
Use the original approved token artwork whenever available. Do not redraw, replace, or materially alter the official mascot asset without Founder approval. New variants should be labeled as variants, not replacements for the original identity.

## 7. Final Statement
The penguin is the character identity of $MYBIE. The sword emblem remains the primary identity of MYBIE SWORD.

**One House. Multiple Sword Coins. One Standard.**
